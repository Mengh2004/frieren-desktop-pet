Visual direction
Recognizable Frieren, chibi 2.2-head proportions, silver-white twin tails, long pointed ears,
jade-green eyes, red teardrop earrings, white/antique-gold clothing and brown boots.
Warm gray outlines, pale lavender hair shadows, quiet green-and-cream accents.

Asset generation
Built-in image_gen tool with transparent_background=true.
Six-cell 3x2 atlas, ordered idle, blink, happy, magic, sleep, read.
The atlas is sliced in memory with WPF CroppedBitmap; no network access at runtime.
Breathing, gentle sway, pat bounce, hearts, magic particles and sleepy z letters are animated in code.
The role and state designs are generated artwork; motion is procedural 2D, not a Live2D rig or 3D model.

Prompt and refinement are saved in assets/generation-prompt.txt.
